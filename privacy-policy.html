Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

Thank you for using myQur’an, a simple, ad-free Qur’an reader and player developed as part of the Maranao Tafsir Project.

1. Information We Collect

myQur’an does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline. It has no user accounts, analytics, or tracking tools.

2. App Permissions

The app may request access to storage only to read or save Qur’an data locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

3. Ads and External Links

myQur’an is 100% ad-free. If the app links to other Qur’an or reference materials online, those external pages have their own privacy policies.

4. Updates

This policy may be updated as the app improves. We encourage you to review it from time to time. Changes will be reflected in this page.
